Specification Item 6

6. Where they dropped off: Snapshot from analytics

Friction point breakdown and the direct 2-click scheduling solution to unlock conversion.

The Friction Barrier

100% Drop-Off at Multi-Field Form Before Calendar Selection

Analytics reveals that while 74 qualified finance leaders read the product value proposition for 42 seconds, zero booked a meeting. Visitors reached the demo request section but encountered an exhaustive questionnaire:
  • Required first name, last name, corporate email, and direct phone.
  • Required annual software budget, company headcount, and TM1 version.
  • No calendar availability was shown prior to form submission.
  • Forced busy CFOs to wait for an asynchronous email follow-up.
⚠ Result: 100% abandonment rate at form submission.
The Immediate Fix

Direct 2-Click Calendar Scheduler

Remove all friction by replacing the questionnaire with an embedded direct calendar scheduler (Calendly / HubSpot Meetings):
  • Click 1: Visitor selects their preferred date on the live calendar.
  • Click 2: Visitor chooses an open 15-minute consultation slot.
  • Only asks for corporate email to deliver the calendar invite.
  • Sends calendar invite directly to Outlook or Google Calendar.
✓ Result: Converts high-intent 42s dwell traffic into qualified sales calls.
