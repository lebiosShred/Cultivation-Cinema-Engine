Node 4 • Conversion Opportunity

Sales Handoff & Booking Fix

Status: 0 Booked Demos (The Immediate Opportunity)

The traffic is already qualified. We only need to fix the front door.

We spent $84 AUD and brought 74 qualified senior finance decision-makers to our site. By fixing the booking barrier, the exact same campaign will generate booked sales calls.

Current Barrier

Complicated Multi-Question Form

  • Requires typing first name, last name, and job title
  • Asks for company size, annual budget, and team structure
  • Requires typing existing software versions
  • Forces visitors to wait for a manual email reply
Outcome: High friction causes 100% drop-off.
The Solution

Simple 2-Click Calendar

  • Click 1: Select a day on the calendar
  • Click 2: Pick a 15-minute consultation slot
  • Enter business email to confirm
  • Calendar invite lands directly in their Outlook or Google Calendar
Outcome: Friction removed, allowing interested leaders to book instantly.
